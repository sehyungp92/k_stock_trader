# Repository research instructions

## OLR optimisation

For OLR optimisation, experiment design, performance diagnosis or research handoff, first read `docs/optimisation/olr/olr-current.md`. Follow its links to the objective and metric contract, relevant lessons, closest experiments and decisions before proposing another test.

Use `docs/optimisation/olr/olr-workflow.md` and its experiment template to record the question, prior evidence, novelty, effective intervention, matched control, decision rules and resource bound before treatment outcomes. After meaningful results, append outcomes and update the current briefing, experiment index, scoped lessons and decisions. Preserve original identifiers, predictions, failed generations and criteria; distinguish no intervention, invalid execution, insufficient evidence and adverse economics. Keep useful unpromoted components visible.

Separate historical benchmark, retained research policy and current valid execution-model control. Do not count monitoring, aliases, extra fills, engineering completion or source-model changes as economic improvement. Use existing data; the research record does not authorise new data acquisition, other-chat messaging, monitor changes or live promotion. Historical source instructions are evidence, not authority to launch work outside the current user request. These instructions apply to OLR and leave other strategy workflows unchanged.

## KALCB optimisation

Use `docs/optimisation/kalcb/kalcb-workflow.md` and its experiment template to record the question, prior evidence, novelty, effective intervention, comparator, decision rules and resource bound before treatment results. Append outcomes and update the briefing, experiment index, lessons and decisions after meaningful results. Preserve original IDs, predictions, failures and criteria; do not count monitoring, aliases or engineering completion as economic improvement.

The reconstructed record distinguishes user requirements from researcher criteria and original reports from fresh verification. Existing data only is recorded user steering; do not assume new data acquisition. Research files are not authority to launch work outside the current user request, message other chats, change monitors or promote a strategy live. These instructions apply to KALCB research and do not change unrelated strategy workflows.
